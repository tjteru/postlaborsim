Methodology & Assumptions

▼

Monte Carlo Approach

This simulation uses Monte Carlo methods to model the economic impact of AI adoption across sectors. The model incorporates stochastic variables for adoption rates, productivity gains, and employment displacement effects.

Key Model Equations

  • GDP Growth: ΔY = α × A × P × (1 + ε × L)
  • Employment Change: ΔL = -D × E × A × (1 + η)
  • Productivity Impact: P = base_productivity × multiplier × exposure
  • Sectoral Aggregation: Total = Σ(sector_weight × sector_impact)

Parameter Definitions

  • A: AI Adoption Rate (5%-80%)
  • D: Job Displacement Rate (5%-40%)
  • P: Productivity Multiplier (0.5x-2.0x)
  • ε: Demand Elasticity (-1.5 to -0.3)
  • E: Sector AI Exposure (sector-specific)
  • η: Random shock (normally distributed)
